# Rafiq landing page surface brief

## Scope and mode

- Target: `src/app/page.tsx`
- Visitor mode: Persuade
- Scope: Live Android acquisition landing page plus paths into legal/support content.

## Audience, job, and action

The visitor is a Muslim Android user evaluating whether Rafiq can help interrupt adult content, short-video feeds, and app overuse without exporting sensitive browsing context. Their primary action is to open the live Google Play listing and install Rafiq.

## Proof and content

Use only real Rafiq screens, the confirmed on-device mechanism, strict/offline/reboot behavior already present in product copy, Qur'an 24:30 with translation/reference, the public Google Play listing, and the exact privacy/legal documents. There are no testimonials, ratings, user counts, or benchmarks to add to the site.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Focused conversion narrative in the incumbent “Steadfast Companion” visual world. The first viewport pairs a plain, emotionally direct promise and direct Google Play action with two real phone screens. The compact three-state pause demonstration lets the visitor control how the screen changes instead of pinning several scroll viewports.

## Constraints

- No waitlist, newsletter, or email-capture form.
- Every acquisition action uses the exact public Google Play listing and a recognisable Play icon.
- No pricing, auto modal, exit intent, countdown, invented proof, or urgency theater.
- All content and direct store links remain available without JavaScript.
- Motion uses one library, remains restrained, and never establishes content visibility.
- The internal acquisition section is `#download`; primary CTAs lead directly to Google Play.
- Custom-domain connection remains out of scope.

- A custom production domain will be selected and connected later.
