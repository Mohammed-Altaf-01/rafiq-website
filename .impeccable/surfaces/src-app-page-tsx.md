# Rafiq landing page surface brief

## Scope and mode

- Target: `src/app/page.tsx`
- Visitor mode: Persuade
- Scope: Pre-launch Android acquisition landing page plus paths into legal/support content.

## Audience, job, and action

The visitor is a Muslim Android user evaluating whether Rafiq can help interrupt adult content, short-video feeds, and app overuse without exporting sensitive browsing context. Their primary action is to submit an email before launch and reserve 30 days of full Premium with no card.

## Proof and content

Use only real Rafiq screens, the confirmed on-device mechanism, strict/offline/reboot behavior already present in product copy, Qur'an 24:30 with translation/reference, and the exact privacy/legal documents. The Google Form response Sheet is the eligibility record; the launch email provides redemption instructions. There are no testimonials, ratings, user counts, benchmarks, or public launch-date claims.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Focused conversion narrative in the incumbent “Steadfast Companion” visual world. The first viewport pairs a plain, emotionally direct promise and visible email claim with two real phone screens. The compact three-state pause demonstration lets the visitor control how the screen changes instead of pinning several scroll viewports.

## Constraints

- Two synchronized forms at most: hero and free-month offer.
- No pricing, auto modal, exit intent, countdown, invented proof, or urgency theater.
- All content and native form submission remain available without JavaScript.
- Motion uses one library, remains restrained, and never establishes content visibility.
- Navigation and closing actions lead to `#free-month`.
- Custom-domain connection and entitlement automation remain out of scope.

## Unresolved decisions

- A custom production domain will be selected and connected later.
- The Android launch operation must implement the actual 30-day entitlement and send redemption instructions from the eligibility Sheet.
