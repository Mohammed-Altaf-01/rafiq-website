## 4. The Accessibility permission

Rafiq's blocking depends on Android's Accessibility Service, and we take that responsibility seriously.

**What it is for.** Rafiq uses Accessibility to detect adult content, to detect short-video feeds like YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, to detect the sites you chose to block, to notice when you leave and return to a blocked app so a pause behaves correctly, and to warn you before a setting that protects you is changed.

**What it reads.** Which app is open, and the text, interface labels and screen layout visible in supported browsers, video and social apps, and in the Android settings and uninstall screens Rafiq watches. In a browser this includes the web address, search text, and visible page text. In a supported video or social app it can include any text on screen. It ignores password fields. Because Accessibility runs while you use other apps, this happens even when Rafiq is not open.

**What it never does.** Rafiq never stores, logs, transmits, sells or shares any address, search term or screen text. Matching happens in memory on your device and the text is discarded immediately. If a block fires, what is recorded is its category, its time, and whether you chose to continue past it — never what triggered it.

**Time limits are measured separately.** Per-app time limits are counted through Android's Usage Access permission, not through Accessibility.

**Your control.** Rafiq asks for this permission on a dedicated screen that describes exactly what is above, with **Agree** and **Not now** side by side. Choosing **Not now** leaves Accessibility off and lets you keep using the rest of Rafiq; you can turn protection on later from the Protect tab. Nothing is read until you have both agreed in the app and switched the service on in Android Settings, and you can switch it off again at any time.

## 5. How blocking works

- **Free tier** — blocks still appear, but they are **soft**: after a brief pause you can choose to continue.
- **Premium and trial** — blocks are **strict**: adult-content blocks hold for a timer you set, app-limit blocks hold until midnight, and short-video blocks require you to leave the feed. An optional PIN adds friction to bypassing them.

Rafiq never permanently locks you out. Every block eventually offers a way forward — a timer, an exit, or the next day — which protects you against false positives and honors your autonomy.

Blocking relies on curated lists and heuristics. We cannot guarantee that every harmful thing is caught or that there are never false positives, and detection for Shorts and Reels can break when those apps change (we push fixes without needing an app update). Rafiq works offline using a bundled list; updates need a connection.

## 7. Prayer times and reminders

Salah preparation is optional and off until you turn it on.

**Prayer times are calculated, not authoritative.** Calculation methods genuinely differ between
authorities, and reasonable scholars disagree about them. Rafiq lets you choose the method, the Asr
convention and a per-prayer adjustment, and defaults to the one commonly followed in your region — but
the times it shows are a calculation, not a ruling. **Rafiq calculates the beginning of the prayer
window. It is not a mosque's iqamah or congregation time**, and it should not be used as one.

**We do not guarantee accuracy.** Times may be wrong because of a location that has gone stale, a
method that does not match your local practice, or a provider response we could not verify. At very
high latitudes there are periods when no method produces a valid time at all, and Rafiq will say so
rather than invent one. Please verify against your local mosque or a trusted local timetable.

**We do not guarantee delivery.** The reminder relies on an exact alarm, and Android manufacturers
apply their own battery and background policies. A reminder may be delayed or may not arrive — because
the device was asleep, the permission was withdrawn, or the manufacturer's power manager stopped the
app. Rafiq is a reminder, not a substitute for your own attention to prayer times.

**The prayer-times service.** When location or city mode is used, your approximate location is sent to
AlAdhan, a third party, to obtain a schedule. AlAdhan publishes no availability or accuracy warranty,
and Rafiq cross-checks every day it returns against an on-device calculation before using it.
